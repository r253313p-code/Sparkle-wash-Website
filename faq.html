Frequently asked questions

How long does a wash take?

An express wash takes about 20 minutes. A full detail can take up to 3 hours.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booked customers are served first.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Call us at least 2 hours before your slot.

How do I pay?

Pay after the wash by cash or mobile money.

Do you wash vehicles in the rain?

Yes. Interior services run in any weather.
